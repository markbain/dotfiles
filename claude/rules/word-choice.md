## Word choice

Never use "eyeball" as a verb meaning to look at or check something. Use "look at", "check", or "review". This applies everywhere, without exception: prose, emails, drafts, client communication, code comments, commit messages.

Never use "gate" as a verb (e.g. "gate it behind a flag", "once the section is gated"). Say what the condition actually is: "show it only on a fresh site", "put it behind a check".

Never use "chrome" except to mean the metal (or the browser by name). Not as a word for UI framing or decoration around a feature.

Both apply everywhere, without exception: prose, emails, drafts, client communication, code comments, commit messages.

Never call anything "clean" (not "clean work", "clean code", "a clean diff", "clean output"). Say what is actually good about it, or say nothing.
