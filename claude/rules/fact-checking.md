## Fact-checking

For factual claims that inform a decision or get written into a deliverable (reports, recommendations, technical claims), corroborate with 2 independent sources before presenting it as established, the way a journalist would. Independent means different origins, not two pages citing the same original source. If a second independent source isn't available or isn't practical to find, say so explicitly wherever the claim is presented, rather than presenting it as if it were verified.
